Compare Futures Prop Firms in 2026: Side-by-Side Analysis

Choosing the right futures trading prop firm requires a detailed look at drawdown calculation methods, profit split percentages, payout frequency, daily loss limits (DLL), and consistency rules. Our independent comparison tool tracks over 80+ active evaluation and instant-funding plans across the 10 top industry leaders.

01. EOD Trailing vs. Intraday Trailing Drawdown

The most critical rule when you compare futures prop firms is the maximum loss calculation. End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Drawdown only updates your maximum trailing limit at the official market close based on your realized balance. Firms like Topstep, Tradeify, and Lucid Trading use EOD trailing, giving traders breathing room during intraday fluctuations. Conversely, Intraday Trailing Drawdown ticks up with your highest unrealized open profit in real-time.

02. Profit Splits & Payout Terms

Modern futures prop trading programs offer industry-leading profit splits ranging from 80% to 100%. For instance, Apex Trader Funding offers 100% on the first $25,000 earned per account, followed by a 90% split thereafter. Topstep provides a 90% profit split right from your first payout, while firms like FundedNext and Lucid Trading deliver rapid payouts within hours or days of request once minimum trading criteria are satisfied.

03. Consistency Rules & News Trading

Before starting an evaluation, verify whether a firm enforces best-day consistency caps (e.g., no single trading day exceeding 30% or 50% of your total profits). If your strategy relies on macroeconomic releases, filter for firms with News Trading OK, or review our detailed breakdown on futures prop firm rules and drawdown limits to ensure compatibility with your trading style.
