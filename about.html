About Us

We help traders find
the right prop firm

Futures Prop Hub is 100% independent. We are not owned by, partnered with, or employed by any prop firm. Every rating, ranking, and review on this site is based on publicly verifiable data and our own hands-on research — with zero pressure from advertisers to skew results.

Our Mission

Three principles that guide everything

We founded this site because finding reliable, unbiased information about futures prop firms was frustratingly hard. These three commitments shape every decision we make.

🔍

Independent Reviews

We test accounts ourselves, read user feedback across dozens of trading communities, and cross-reference official firm documentation. No firm can buy a higher rating. If data changes, so does the score — immediately.

📊

Real Data Only

Every data point — account sizes, fees, drawdown rules, payout ratios, profit splits — is sourced from official firm pages, terms of service, or direct user verification. We cite our sources and flag anything that can't be confirmed.

🎯

Trader-First

We design every feature — the comparison table, the head-to-head tool, the filters — for traders, not firms. If a feature doesn't help you make a better decision, it doesn't make the site. Full stop.

Transparency

How we make money

We believe transparency about our business model is non-negotiable. Here's the honest picture.

💡 Affiliate Disclosure

We earn affiliate commissions — and it never changes our ratings

Some links on Futures Prop Hub are affiliate links. This means if you click through and purchase an evaluation account from a prop firm, we may receive a referral commission from that firm at no extra cost to you.

We only list firms that we have genuinely researched and believe are legitimate options for traders. Affiliate relationships do not influence our ratings, rankings, or editorial content. Firms that pay higher commissions do not rank higher. A firm with no affiliate program is reviewed and rated exactly the same as one that does.

If you would like to support our work without spending anything extra, simply use our links when you decide to sign up. We appreciate it — it keeps the site running and independent.

Data Policy

How we handle firm data

Keeping our data accurate is one of our most important responsibilities. Here's exactly how we do it.

🗓️

Regular Updates

We review and refresh all firm data on a rolling basis. When a firm changes its fees, rules, or payout structure, we update the relevant pages as soon as we become aware of the change.

🔗

Primary Sources First

Whenever possible, data is pulled directly from official firm websites, FAQs, and legal terms. We flag any data points that rely on user-reported information rather than official sources.

✉️

Report an Error

Found something wrong? Please contact us directly. We treat every data correction report seriously and aim to verify and update within 48 hours.

⚠️

No Guarantee of Completeness

Prop firm terms change frequently and sometimes without notice. We do our best, but always verify critical details — especially fee structures and drawdown rules — directly with the firm before purchasing.
