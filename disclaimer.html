Data Accuracy Disclaimer

We invest significant effort in researching, verifying, and maintaining the accuracy of all firm data, pricing, rules, and program details published on this site. Our review process includes direct review of firm websites, published rulebooks, and community-sourced feedback.

However, prop firm rules, pricing, evaluation structures, payout policies, and platform features change frequently — sometimes without public notice. The futures prop trading industry is fast-moving, and information that is accurate today may become outdated tomorrow.
