What to Look For When Comparing Futures Prop Firms

When doing a futures prop firm comparison, five factors matter most:

1. Drawdown Type — The Most Important Rule

The drawdown rule determines how much you can lose before your account is closed. There are three types:

  • Static drawdown — Fixed from your starting balance. Never tightens. Used by Topstep, Lucid, Alpha Futures. Best for traders who build profits quickly because the floor never chases you.
  • Trailing EOD (End-of-Day) — Locks in each night at your highest daily close. Used by Apex, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader. The drawdown floor moves up as you profit, but only once per day.
  • Trailing intraday — Locks in tick-by-tick at your highest unrealized balance. The most restrictive — your buffer shrinks as you go in-profit during the session.
💡 Key insight: Scalpers and news traders who go in-profit quickly during a session are disproportionately hurt by intraday trailing drawdown. If that's your style, prioritise firms with static or EOD trailing drawdown.

2. Daily Loss Limit

A daily loss limit (DLL) stops you from trading once you've lost a set amount in a single day. Firms with no DLL — like Topstep, Take Profit Trader, and Funded Seat — give you more flexibility on volatile news days. If you trade around FOMC or NFP, this is a critical filter.

3. Profit Split

Most futures prop firms offer 80–90% profit splits on funded accounts. Topstep, Apex, and MyFundedFutures offer 90%. Some firms offer a path to 100% after consistent performance. Don't choose a firm based on profit split alone — a 90% split is worthless if the drawdown rules make it impossible to stay funded.

4. Payout Speed

Payout speeds range from daily (Tradeify, Take Profit Trader) to 14 days (Apex, FundedNext). If consistent cash flow matters to you — especially if you're trading full-time — payout frequency should be high on your checklist when comparing futures prop firms.

5. Evaluation Cost & Promotions

Evaluation prices vary significantly. Funded Seat starts around $80 for a 50K account. Apex and Tradeify run frequent promotions of 60–90% off. Use promo code FPC40 for 40% off most firms, or FPC90 for 90% off Apex Trader Funding.

Futures Prop Firm Comparison: Key Rules Explained

What is a consistency rule?

Some firms require that no single trading day accounts for more than 30–40% of your total profits. This prevents traders from "spiking" one day and withdrawing. Firms with no consistency rule — like Topstep — give you full flexibility to have one big day and immediately withdraw.

What is an activation fee?

Several firms charge a one-time "activation fee" when you receive your funded account, separate from the evaluation cost. This is a common hidden cost. Always check a firm's funded account terms — not just the evaluation price — before signing up.

What is a scaling plan?

Many firms let you increase your account size as you hit profit milestones. Apex offers scaling to $300K+. Topstep allows scaling through their Step Up program. If growing your account size is a goal, compare the scaling terms alongside the base rules.
