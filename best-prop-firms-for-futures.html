What Makes a Futures Prop Firm "The Best"?

The best futures prop firm for you depends on five factors:

  1. Drawdown type — Static is the most trader-friendly. Intraday trailing is the most restrictive.
  2. Daily loss limit — No DLL gives you flexibility on bad days and around news events.
  3. Payout speed — Daily vs 14 days makes a massive difference if you're trading full-time.
  4. News trading permission — If you trade around FOMC, NFP, or CPI, most firms will invalidate your trades.
  5. Cost — Factor in the evaluation price, any reset fees, and the activation fee on the funded account.
💡 Pro tip: Never choose a prop firm based on profit split alone. A 90% split is useless if the drawdown rules make it near-impossible to stay funded. Focus on the drawdown structure first, everything else second.
